About the Band

Welcome to the official home of Far From Hibernia! We are a traditional Irish traditional music outfit bringing the soaring melodies, driving rhythm, and timeless storytelling of Celtic heritage straight to local pubs and regional festivals.

Combining authentic multi-instrumental traditional tunes, driving sea shanties, and lively pub songs, our performances blend historical reverence with infectious acoustic energy designed to get rooms moving. Sláinte!
